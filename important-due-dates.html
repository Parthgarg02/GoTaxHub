Important Due Dates

Stay compliant with all tax and regulatory deadlines for Tax Year 2026-27 (assessed in AY 2027-28)

Never miss a deadline again! Keep track of all important compliance dates for Income Tax, GST, TDS, Company Law, and more.

Reviewed as at 12 November 2026. Dates shown are for Tax Year 2026-27 and reflect the Income-tax Act, 2025, in force from 1 April 2026. Deadlines can be extended by CBDT/CBIC notification — please confirm before relying on any date here.

⏰ Due in the Coming Weeks

November 30, 2026 ITR filing — transfer pricing cases (FY 2025-26)
December 15, 2026 Advance tax — third instalment (75% cumulative)
December 31, 2026 Belated and revised ITR for FY 2025-26
December 31, 2026 GST annual return (GSTR-9 / 9C) for FY 2025-26
January 31, 2027 Quarterly TDS return for Oct–Dec 2026
March 15, 2027 Advance tax — fourth and final instalment (100%)

📄 Income Tax Returns

July 31, 2027 ITR Filing (Individuals, non-audit) - FY 2026-27
August 31, 2027 ITR Filing (business & professional, non-audit — ITR-3/4) - Tax Year 2026-27
October 31, 2027 ITR Filing (Audit Cases) - FY 2026-27
October 31, 2027 ITR Filing (Companies) - FY 2026-27

🏢 Company Law Compliance

October 30, 2027 Annual Return Filing (Form AOC-4 & MGT-7)
May 30 / October 30, 2027 Annual Filing for LLPs (Form 11 & Form 8)
May 30, 2027 Board Meeting for Annual Accounts

📊 GST Compliance

11th of Every Month GSTR-1 Filing (Monthly)
20th of Every Month GSTR-3B Filing & Payment
13th of Next Month GSTR-1 Quarterly (Small Taxpayers)
Before first export of the FY Letter of Undertaking (Form RFD-11) — annual, cannot be backdated

💰 TDS & TCS Compliance

7th of Every Month TDS Payment (Government/Non-Government)
Jul 31 / Oct 31 / Jan 31 / May 31 Quarterly TDS Return (Form 138 / 140 — previously 24Q / 26Q)
June 15, 2027 Salary TDS Certificate (Form 130 — previously Form 16)

🏦 Audit & Assessment

September 30, 2027 Tax Audit Report Filing - FY 2026-27
December 31, 2027 GST Annual Return (GSTR-9)
August 31, 2027 Statutory Audit Report Submission

📋 Other Important Dates

March 31, 2027 Financial Year End & Investment Deadline
June 15, 2026 Advance Tax Payment (Q1)
September 15, 2026 Advance Tax Payment (Q2)
December 15, 2026 Advance Tax Payment (Q3)
March 15, 2027 Advance Tax Payment (Q4)
