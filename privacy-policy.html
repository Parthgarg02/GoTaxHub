Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities

Last Updated: 22 September 2026

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Introduction

GoTaxHub ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • PAN number, Aadhaar number (as required for tax services)
  • Financial information including income details, bank statements
  • Tax-related documents and forms
  • Address and contact information

2.2 Technical Information

  • IP address, browser type, and version
  • Device information and operating system
  • Website usage data and analytics
  • Cookies and tracking technologies

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide tax preparation and filing services
  • Process GST registrations and compliance
  • Offer business advisory and consultation services
  • Communicate about your service requests
  • Send important updates about tax law changes
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3A. Cookies and Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our pages. Google Analytics sets cookies that record pages viewed, approximate location and device type. We use this only in aggregate, to improve the site. We do not use advertising or profiling cookies.

You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on, without losing access to any part of this website.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Access to client records limited to the people working on your file
  • Encrypted connections for data transmission
  • Secure servers with access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Employee training on data protection

5. Information Sharing

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With government authorities for tax filing purposes
  • With our certified CA partners for service delivery
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations — currently Formspree (contact form delivery) and Google Analytics (website usage statistics)
  • When required by law or legal proceedings
  • With your explicit consent

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for the following periods:

  • Tax documents: 7 years (as per income tax regulations)
  • Personal information: Until service completion plus 3 years
  • Communication records: 2 years
  • Website analytics: 2 years

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Request data portability
  • File complaints with regulatory authorities

8. Cookies Policy

We use cookies to:

  • Maintain your session and preferences
  • Analyze website usage and performance
  • Provide personalized content
  • Enable security features

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Updates to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or website notice. Your continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Compliance

We comply with:

  • Information Technology Act, 2000
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the rules made under it
  • The Income-tax Act, 2025 (in force from 1 April 2026) and the Income-tax Act, 1961 for earlier periods
  • The Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and allied GST legislation
  • The Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011, so far as they continue to apply

12. Grievance Officer and Contact

If you have a question or complaint about how your personal data is handled, write to our Grievance Officer. We will acknowledge your request and respond within the period required under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
